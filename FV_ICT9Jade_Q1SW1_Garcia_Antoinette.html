About My Music Taste

I like listening to different kinds of music depending on my mood. Ariana Grande, Denise Julia, and The Weeknd are three of my favorite artists because their songs have catchy melodies, different vibes, and lyrics that I can enjoy while studying, relaxing, or just listening to music.

Ariana Grande

I like Ariana Grande because of her beautiful voice and her pop and R&B songs. Her music can be calm, fun, and emotional, which makes her songs enjoyable to listen to.

Denise Julia

I like Denise Julia because of her modern R&B sound and catchy songs. Her music has a smooth vibe that makes it easy to listen to, especially when relaxing.

The Weeknd

I like The Weeknd because his songs have a unique sound and strong atmosphere. His music is perfect when I want something chill, catchy, or more emotional.
